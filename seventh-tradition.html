Information about the Seventh Tradition and how it applies to our meeting. Non members don't contribute.

The Seventh Tradition

"Every () group ought to be fully self-supporting, declining outside contributions."

How Contributions Work

1. Meeting Expenses: Rent for the room, coffee, tea, and AA literature. 2. Support Services: Contributions sent to local central offices and the General Service Office (GSO) to help print books and run websites.

Participation

1. Self-Reliance: Groups pay their own way. They do not accept money from non-members, charities, or corporations. 2. Independence: Refusing outside money prevents donors from influencing AA's message or policies. 3. No Dues or Fees: Membership is completely free. Contributing money is entirely voluntary. 4. Spiritual Connection: Giving back is viewed as a form of gratitude and responsibility that aids in personal recovery.
